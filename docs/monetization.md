# Dali Secure QR — Monetization Plan

## 1. Strategy

Dali Secure QR should avoid charging users for the fundamental functions that make the ecosystem useful.

The core principle is:

> **QR is free. Security is free. Personalization is paid.**

An even broader product principle is:

> **Don't monetize trust. Monetize identity.**

Users should never have to pay simply to determine whether a supported QR destination passes Dali's verification mechanism.

Likewise, basic QR creation should remain free because every QR created and distributed can introduce another person to Dali Secure QR.

---

## 2. Why Not Use a 30-Day Trial?

A traditional model would be:

> Free for one month → subscription required

This is not ideal for Dali Secure QR.

QR scanning and creation are intermittent activities. A user might install the application, use it several times, and not need it again for several weeks.

When they return, discovering that the scanner or security functionality has expired creates unnecessary friction.

More importantly, Dali Secure QR benefits from widespread adoption.

The business objective should initially be:

```text
More creators
     ↓
More Dali Secure QR codes
     ↓
More people receive them
     ↓
More people scan them
     ↓
More Dali Secure QR users
```

A time-based trial interferes with this cycle.

Therefore:

**No expiration should apply to basic QR creation, scanning, or security verification.**

---

# 3. Free Core Product

The free version should be a genuinely useful QR application rather than a crippled trial.

## Scanning

Free users can:

- Scan standard QR codes
- Decode URLs
- Read text/message QR codes
- Recognize email, phone, SMS, location, Wi-Fi, contact, calendar, and other supported formats
- Preview content before executing an action
- Open supported content through the operating system

## Secure QR Verification

Free users can:

- Scan Dali Secure QR
- See domain-based verification information for supported web URLs
- Compare the security code
- See the registrable domain
- Receive warnings when verification fails

Security verification should **never require payment**.

## QR Creation

Free users can:

- Create QR codes
- Create text/message QR codes
- Create URL QR codes
- Create Dali Secure QR for supported URLs
- Save generated QR codes
- Share generated QR codes
- Print generated QR codes

There should be no arbitrary quota such as:

```text
5 QR codes per month
```

or:

```text
20 lifetime QR codes
```

Unlimited basic creation encourages distribution.

# 4. Paid Feature: Center Emblem

The primary consumer monetization feature should be customization of the QR center.

In this plan, **custom center emblem** means artwork supplied by the user, such
as a personal image or organization logo. The built-in Dali icon and built-in
verification-code badge remain free to export because they support recognition,
verification, and distribution of the Dali format.

A standard free QR may look like:

```text
┌───────────────────────┐
│ ██  ████   ██   ████ │
│ ██     █ █ █      ██ │
│ ████ ███   ████ ███ │
│   █ ██   ███ █ █     │
│ ███ █████  █ ██ ███ │
└───────────────────────┘
```

A premium QR can contain a center emblem:

```text
┌───────────────────────┐
│ ██  ████   ██   ████ │
│ ██     █ █ █      ██ │
│ ████   ┌─────┐ █ ███│
│   █ ██ │ LOGO│ █     │
│ ███ █  └─────┘ ██ ███│
└───────────────────────┘
```

Examples include:

- Personal image
- Company logo
- Church logo
- Organization emblem
- Event symbol
- Product logo
- Artwork
- Initials
- Custom icon

This changes the purchase decision from:

> "Pay to use QR."

to:

> "Pay to make this QR mine."

That is a much healthier monetization boundary.

---

# 5. Preview Before Purchase

Users should be allowed to design their premium QR before purchasing.

Workflow:

```text
Create QR
    ↓
Choose "Add Center Emblem"
    ↓
Select image/logo
    ↓
See complete QR preview
    ↓
Scan-test QR
    ↓
Save / Export
    ↓
Unlock required
```

This has several advantages.

The user sees the value before purchasing.

Instead of selling an abstract feature called:

> Custom Center Emblem

Dali is effectively showing:

> Here is the QR you just created. Unlock it if you want to use this version.

The user can still remove the emblem and export the standard version for free.

---

# 6. Recommended Consumer Pricing

The initial consumer product should use a **one-time lifetime unlock**.

Recommended initial range:

> **$2.99–$4.99 lifetime**

A reasonable launch point would be:

> **$3.99 — Custom QR Lifetime Unlock**

Possible App Store / Play Store product:

```text
Dali Secure QR Custom
One-Time Purchase
$3.99
```

The unlock should apply to the user's consumer customization features rather than individual QR codes.

Do not charge:

```text
$0.99 per QR
```

Per-QR pricing would discourage experimentation and sharing.

# 8. Security Must Never Be Premium

This should be an explicit Dali product policy.

Dali should not create situations such as:

> "Upgrade to see whether this QR is safe."

or:

> "Your security verification trial has expired."

That would undermine the purpose of Dali Secure QR.

Instead:

```text
Scan
  ↓
Verification
  ↓
Always Free
```

Security should help drive adoption rather than serve as the paywall.

---

# 9. QR Creation Should Also Remain Free

Free creation is strategically important.

Consider a user creating a poem:

```text
《水調歌頭・方寸辨真》
```

They put the entire poem directly into a QR code and send it to a friend.

The friend scans it.

That interaction becomes:

```text
Creator
   ↓
Creates QR
   ↓
Shares QR
   ↓
Recipient
   ↓
Scans QR
   ↓
Discovers Dali Secure QR
```

Every free QR can therefore function as a small distribution mechanism for Dali.

Charging for creation would reduce this effect.

# 10. QR as an Envelope

Dali Secure QR should not be positioned only as a website-link tool.

A useful concept is:

> **Sometimes the QR itself is the envelope.**

Users can put information directly inside QR codes:

- Poems
- Greetings
- Blessings
- Personal messages
- Instructions
- Contact information
- Wi-Fi information
- Event information
- Short stories
- Notes

For example:

> **這首詞,不寫在紙上。**  
> **掃一下,我送給你。**

followed by the QR.

This gives Dali Secure QR a creative use case beyond conventional QR generators.

These uses should remain free because they encourage people to create and exchange QR codes.

# 13. Future Customization Features

Once center-emblem customization proves useful, the same lifetime unlock could include additional appearance features.

Possible features include:

- Logo placement
- Emblem shapes
- QR frame styles
- Text underneath QR
- Organization name
- Custom caption
- Higher-resolution export
- Print layouts
- QR templates

These should be introduced carefully.

QR reliability must always take priority over appearance.

Every customized QR should be validated after rendering.

Recommended workflow:

```text
Generate QR
    ↓
Apply customization
    ↓
Render final image
    ↓
Decode final image internally
    ↓
Compare decoded payload
    ↓
PASS → Allow export

FAIL → Adjust customization
```

This is particularly important for center emblems because they cover part of the QR matrix.

# 18. Guiding Principles

Dali Secure QR monetization should follow five rules:

### 1. Don't monetize trust.

Security verification remains free.

### 2. Don't punish distribution.

Basic QR creation remains unlimited and free.

### 3. Monetize identity.

Logos, center emblems, branding, and customization are premium.

### 4. Let users see value before paying.

Allow complete customization previews before requiring the unlock.

### 5. Charge recurring fees only for recurring value.

Consumer customization should use a lifetime purchase.

Future organization services can use subscriptions when they provide continuing management, analytics, or infrastructure.

# 19. Recommended Launch Model

For the initial release:

**Dali Secure QR — Free**

Includes:

- Unlimited QR scanning
- Unlimited basic QR generation
- Text/message QR
- URL QR
- Secure QR generation
- Domain verification
- Other supported QR payload types
- Save and share

**Dali Secure QR Custom — $3.99 Lifetime Unlock**

Adds:

- Center emblem
- Custom image/logo
- Premium QR appearance features
- Customized QR export

No subscription.

No 30-day expiration.

No charge for security verification.

No QR creation quota.

## Initial Implementation

The application uses one non-consumable store product:

```text
dali_secure_qr_custom_lifetime
```

The initial implementation follows these rules:

- Users may select an image and generate a complete custom QR preview for free.
- Copying, sharing, and history persistence of that custom output require the
  lifetime entitlement.
- Plain QR output, the Dali icon, and the verification-code badge remain free.
- Every decorated QR is decoded internally and compared with the source payload
  before export. The emblem area is reduced automatically when necessary.
- The localized price comes from the active store; no price is hard-coded in
  the application.
- Purchase restoration is available from the unlock screen.
- Scanned and generated payload content is never sent to the purchase provider.

---

# 20. Core Business Philosophy

The strongest monetization principle for Dali Secure QR is:

> **Don't monetize trust. Monetize identity.**

The more people who can freely create, share, scan, and verify Dali Secure QR codes, the more valuable the ecosystem becomes.

The paid upgrade should therefore make users' QR codes **more personal, recognizable, and professional** without making the free QR codes less functional or less secure.
